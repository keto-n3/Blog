TRAVEL

Discovering Nariño

La Cocha Lagoon and the beauty of Nariño

Nariño is a beautiful department in southern Colombia. It has amazing landscapes, interesting traditions, delicious food and many places to explore. One of the places I would like to highlight is La Cocha Lagoon, located near El Encano. It is known for its beautiful natural landscape and peaceful environment. It is a great place to appreciate nature, enjoy the scenery and learn more about the culture of the region. Nariño also has an interesting gastronomy. Traditional food is an important part of the region's culture and identity. For me, Nariño is a special place because it has many things to explore. Its landscapes, culture, gastronomy and people make it a region worth discovering.

FORMATIVE COMPONENT

Critical Thinking and Media Literacy

Thinking before believing and sharing

Critical thinking is the ability to analyze information carefully before accepting it as true. Media literacy is the ability to access, understand, evaluate and create information through different types of media. Today, people receive information from social media, websites, television, videos and other digital platforms. Not all information is reliable or accurate, so it is important to verify information before believing or sharing it. We should check the source, compare information from different sources, check the publication date and look for evidence.

FORMATIVE COMPONENT

Crime and Punishment

Laws, justice and responsibility

Crime is an action that violates the law. Different countries have different laws and punishments depending on the type and seriousness of the crime. Laws are important because they establish rules for society and help protect people's rights. Justice systems use laws and evidence to determine responsibility and apply appropriate legal consequences. Understanding crime and punishment can help us understand the importance of laws, responsibility and justice in society.
